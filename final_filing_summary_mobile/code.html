1

Login to Income Tax Portal

Visit incometax.gov.in and login using your PAN and password.

2

File Return

Navigate to e-File > Income Tax Returns > File Income Tax Return. Select AY 2024-25 and ITR-1.

3

Pre-filled Data & Schedules

Confirm pre-filled data. Manually enter the summarized values from Schedule Salary, HP, and VI-A above.

4

Verify Taxes Paid

Check 'Taxes Paid' against Form 26AS/AIS. Ensure TDS matches our summary.

5

Preview & Submit

Preview the return, proceed to validation, and submit using Aadhaar OTP or EVC.
